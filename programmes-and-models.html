Programs and Models

Three Models. One Trusted System. Start Where It Suits You.

Every city has unique demand, and every investor has a different asset capability. UGC offers three structured campus models, allowing you to launch at Model A and expand over time or establish a full-scale professional campus from day one.

Detailed Model Breakdown

Model A

Intermediate Campus

  • Ideal For: Smaller towns, or initial market testing.
  • Proposed Academic Programs: Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS (Computer Science), I.Com (Commerce), and General Science.
  • Affiliation Path: Proposed affiliation with local government intermediate education boards.
  • Strategic Benefit: Lowest initial setup capital with a fast timeline to operational launch.
Model B

Intermediate + Degree Campus

  • Ideal For: Growing urban centers and regional hubs with demand for higher education.
  • Proposed Academic Programs: All Model A Intermediate streams plus 2-year Associate Degree Programs (ADP) and 4-year BS Undergraduate Degrees.
  • Affiliation Path: Proposed affiliation with local public-sector universities.
  • Strategic Benefit: Extended student retention across 4-6 years, driving higher lifetime revenue per student.
Model C

Full Campus with Professional Programs

  • Ideal For: Major divisional cities (Multan, Bahawalpur, Dera Ghazi Khan) aiming for a full-scale institution.
  • Proposed Academic Programs: All Model A & B streams plus specialized professional fields:
    • Computer Science & Information Technology (CS/IT)
    • Business Administration & Commerce
    • Law
    • Engineering & Technology
    • Pharmacy
    • Nursing
  • Affiliation Path: Proposed affiliation with local public-sector universities and relevant professional councils.
  • Strategic Benefit: Maximum enrollment capacity, broader geographic catchment area, and highest revenue potential.

Regulatory Compliance Statement

All programs listed across Model A, Model B, and Model C are proposed offerings and will be launched strictly following formal approvals from relevant government education boards and public-sector universities. UGC is an independent franchise system and does not grant USP degrees or offer USP programs.
