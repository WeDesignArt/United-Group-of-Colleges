Franchise Opportunity

A College Franchise Opportunity Built for Potential Investors

Education stands as one of Pakistan's most resilient, highly respected sectors delivering steady cash flow across economic cycles and deep community influence. UGC enables you to enter this high-demand market without the burden of building an institution from scratch.

The Business Case

  • Steady Demand: Families prioritize quality education in all economic conditions.
  • Brand Authority: Open under an established academic brand rather than an unproven name.
  • Lower Operational Risk: The curriculum frameworks, software systems, training modules, and quality standards are already built.
  • First-Mover Advantage: Secure your territory in underserved towns across South Punjab and expand with a growing national brand.

Division of Responsibilities

What You Provide

  • One-time franchise fee
  • Capital for setup & initial operational costs
  • Suitable building or land asset (owned or leased)
  • Local staff hiring (guided by UGC criteria)
  • On-ground campus management

Campus Models Overview

MODEL A

Intermediate

The entry model focusing on board-affiliated Intermediate streams.

MODEL B

Intermediate + Degree

A growing campus incorporating Associate Degrees and BS programs (proposed).

MODEL C

Full Campus

A complete campus featuring professional proposed streams (CS/IT, Business, Engineering, Law, Pharmacy, and Nursing).

Turnkey setup of campus management software and IT infrastructure.

Marketing campaigns for admissions.

Regular academic audits and quality reviews.

A dedicated UGC point of contact for ongoing operational queries.

Financial Overview

  • One-Time Franchise Fee

    PKR 2,000,000

  • Total Setup Capital

    Roughly PKR 15,000,000 to 30,000,000 (depending on campus model, city, and building condition)

  • Facility Terms

    Property can be owned or long-term leased.

Note

Investment figures are indicative. Final capital requirements depend on location, physical site scope, and model selection. Revenue is generated mainly through student's tuition fees.

Continuous
Operational Support

UGC partners are never left to manage alone after signing. Every franchisee receives:

  • Dedicated staff recruitment and ongoing faculty training.

  • A complete operational playbook covering daily campus management.

Target Applicant Profile

This opportunity is designed for entrepreneurs, landowners, owners of existing schools/academies and retired professionals who can commit the franchise fee and setup capital to build a long-term enterprise in South Punjab.
