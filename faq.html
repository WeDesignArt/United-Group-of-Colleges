Frequently Asked Questions

Is UGC part of USP? Can it offer USP degrees?

UGC is an independent franchise network powered by the academic standing and governance framework of USP. It is not affiliated with USP for granting qualifications and does not offer USP's internal programs. Qualifications at UGC campuses are awarded through proposed affiliations with government education boards and regional public-sector universities, subject to official approvals.

How much investment is required to open a UGC campus?

Investment varies by campus model, chosen city, and whether you already own suitable land or a building. Our team walks you through the exact breakdown for Model A, Model B, and Model C once you share your city and available asset.

What do I receive for my investment?

You receive the UGC brand, curriculum framework, digital campus management software, staff training, operational playbooks, and ongoing guidance through launch and regulatory approvals.

What do I need to provide as an investor?

You provide the one-time franchise fee, setup capital, and an approved physical building or land asset in your target city.

How are program approvals and affiliations handled?

UGC actively supports your campus through the regulatory approval process with local government education boards and public-sector universities. All programs remain proposed until those approvals are granted.

What ongoing support does UGC provide after launch?

You manage local operations with ongoing staff training, quality audits, marketing strategies, and dedicated guidance from the UGC team behind you.

Where can I open a UGC campus?

UGC is initially focused on the Multan, Bahawalpur, and Dera Ghazi Khan divisions and surrounding towns, with plans to expand the network across Pakistan over time.

Are financial returns or profits guaranteed?

No. Like any business venture, returns depend on local demand, execution, and market conditions. UGC provides the framework and support to reduce risk, but financial outcomes are not guaranteed.

Can I convert an existing school or academy into a UGC campus?

Yes. Owners of existing schools, colleges, or academies can upgrade their institution into a recognized UGC campus, raising academic standards and attracting higher enrollment through brand trust.

How do I start the application process?

Submit your details through the Request Information form. Our team will reach out to walk you through the campus models, investment, and next steps for your city.
